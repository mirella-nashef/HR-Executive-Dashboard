Engagement signals

How these signals are calculated
Consistent under-hours — employees with 5 or more present days under the 9-hour target and an average working day below 9 hours.
High remote (>40%) — employees whose remote days are more than 40% of their working days (remote ÷ (present + remote)).
Burnout risk — employees averaging over 10.5 working hours per present day who took zero annual leave in the range. In the table: High = average above 11h with no leave at all; Medium = average above 10.5h or 5+ days over 11h.
Frequent adjustments — employees with 5 or more attendance adjustment requests.
All counts are numbers of employees, not numbers of days or incidents.
